Who We Are

Tasty Vibes is a food delivery service based in Yangon, Myanmar. We bring together great local and international dishes, from juicy burgers and cheese pizza to Wagyu steak, sushi, Kyay-Oh and refreshing drinks, and deliver them hot and fresh to your door.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make ordering good food easy, fast and enjoyable. We work with talented chefs and use quality ingredients so that every order tastes the way it should.
